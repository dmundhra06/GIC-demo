## Introduction				
This is a data engineering coding interview question.				
The expectation is for the candidate to code up an SQL solution to the case study.				
                
## Case Study				
You are tasked to write a program for batch jobs execution.				
In the execution of a batch job, a series of stored procedures are executed based on a pre-defined sequence.				
                
Each batch job is assigned a unique UNIT_NBR.				
Stored procedures are identified by STEP_PROG_NAME and each stored procedure is assigned a unique STEP_SEQ_ID.				
                
The pre-defined sequence is identified by the mapping of STEP_SEQ_ID and STEP_DEP_ID.				
e.g.	UNIT_NBR	RULE_ID	STEP_SEQ_ID	STEP_DEP_ID
    1	1	1	0
    1	2	2	1
    1	3	3	2
    1	4	4	2
    1	5	5	3
    1	6	5	4
                
From this sequence, STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 1) is the first to start as the STEP_DEP_ID is 0				
STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 2) is the second to trigger as it is dependent on STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 1) to complete				
STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 3,4) will trigger in parallel as they are both dependent on STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 2) to complete				
STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 5) will trigger last after STEP_SEQ_ID (ID 3, 4) both to complete
